Privacy Policy

Ramadan Spirits

Last updated: February 22, 2026

Introduction

Ramadan Spirits ("we", "our", "the app") respects your privacy. This policy explains what data we collect and how we use it.

Data We Collect

Location data: The app uses your device's location to calculate accurate prayer times and Qibla direction. This data is processed locally on your device and is not sent to any server.

Reading progress: Your Quran reading progress, bookmarks, and preferences are stored locally on your device.

Data We Do NOT Collect

We do not collect, store, or share any personal information. Specifically:

- No account or registration required
- No personal data is transmitted to external servers
- No analytics or tracking tools are used
- No advertising SDKs are integrated
- No cookies are used

Third-Party Services

Quran audio: Audio recitations are streamed from mp3quran.net. When you play audio, your device connects to their servers. Please refer to their privacy policy for more information.

Prayer time calculation: Prayer times are calculated locally using the Adhan algorithm. No external API is called for this feature.

Data Storage

All app data (settings, reading progress, bookmarks) is stored locally on your device using standard platform storage. This data is not backed up to any cloud service by the app.

Children's Privacy

The app does not collect any personal data from anyone, including children under 13.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this privacy policy from time to time. Any changes will be reflected on this page with an updated date.
