FAQ

Prayer times are not accurate for my location?
Make sure location services are enabled for the app in your device settings.

Qibla compass is not pointing correctly?
Calibrate your compass by moving your phone in a figure-8 motion.

Audio is not playing?
Ensure you have an active internet connection. Audio is streamed from online servers.
